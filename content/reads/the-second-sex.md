  Overview: This novel includes a variety of speeches written and delivered by
  influential women throughout history. Lunin’s analysis of each speech provides
  the reader with insight on how-to public speak with effectiveness and meaning,
  especially as a woman. 


  Takeaways: 

  - Words have power and can make an impact. 

  - Avoid apologizing/ putting yourself down while speaking- it undermines the influence of your voice. 

  - Passion is power- listeners can see/ hear how you feel about your content.

  - Silence is the only thing that cannot be misunderstood- what is important must be spoken, even - if there is risk it could be misunderstood. 

  - Speeches that disrupt are the ones we remember.
