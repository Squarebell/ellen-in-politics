If you’re learning politics in real time — through classes, internships, feeds, or late-night conversations — you eventually hit a fork: treat equality as optional enrichment, or treat it as core civic literacy.

I choose the second.

Racial equality isn’t a trend board or a once-a-year post. It’s how we measure whether democracy is actually delivering. It’s who feels safe walking home. Who gets the benefit of the doubt. Who gets to imagine a future in the same city as everyone else.

I’m not here with a finished manifesto. I’m here documenting the learning — linking what I read, what I notice in DC, and what I’m still wrestling with. If that resonates, stick around. If you have something to teach me, reach out.
