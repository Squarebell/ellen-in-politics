Politics can feel like something that happens *to* you — a feed of headlines, a scroll of outrage, a cycle that never asks you to step in.

I started writing here because I believe awareness is active. Paying attention is a choice. Sharing what you’re learning is a choice. Reaching out to someone who disagrees with you — carefully, honestly — is a choice.

**Showing up** doesn’t always mean a protest sign or a podium. Sometimes it means reading past the headline. Sometimes it means asking a harder question at dinner. Sometimes it means posting when silence would be easier.

This space is for that kind of showing up: personal, curious, and unfinished on purpose.
