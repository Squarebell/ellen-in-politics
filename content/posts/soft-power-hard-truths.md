There’s a version of feminism that looks great on a mood board and another that lives in policy, paychecks, and who gets interrupted in meetings.

I’m interested in both the aesthetic and the substance — the posters *and* the practice. Equality isn’t soft just because it wears a clean outfit. It’s disciplined. It’s specific. It asks who is missing from the table and why.

If you’re building a career, a campus community, or a public voice, you don’t need permission to care about this. You need clarity, courage, and people who will tell you the truth when the room gets comfortable.

This is one of the threads I’ll keep pulling here: how personal growth and public life braid together for women who want more than a seat — who want a say.
