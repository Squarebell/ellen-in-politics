Washington looks like a postcard until you live inside it.

The Capitol dome at dusk, the Lincoln steps, Georgetown brick — that’s the mood board. Then there’s the metro at 8:12 a.m., the intern badge lines, the hearings you watch on your phone between classes, the quiet realization that policy is made by people who also buy coffee and miss trains.

I want this account — and this site — to hold both: the romance of civic ambition **and** the unglamorous work of staying informed.

If you’re in the District, or dreaming of it, or watching from somewhere else entirely: you’re part of the audience I’m writing for. Awareness travels. Connections do too.
