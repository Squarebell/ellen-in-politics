Spreading awareness without community is a fast way to feel loud and alone.

I’m building in public because I want conversations — with peers, mentors, organizers, classmates, strangers who care about the same fights. Posts are invitations, not performances.

If something here sparks a thought, disagree productively. Share it with someone who should see it. Or just say hello.

Growth doesn’t only happen in silence with a book. Sometimes it happens when you send the first message.
